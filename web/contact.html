Before we start

A few practical questions.

Can I ask about existing software or a technical decision?

Yes. The conversation can cover an existing application, a planned project, an integration, a difficult bug, or choosing between approaches. Linux hosting and infrastructure questions are welcome too.

Does the project need to involve AI?

No. Software consulting and development are the main offer. We consider AI when it is useful for the task, alongside existing tools, straightforward software, and automation.

How does flat-fee pricing work?

Most projects use a flat fee for an agreed scope. We define the work, the price, and how we will review the result before starting. If the scope changes, we agree on any additional work and cost before proceeding.

What follows the free initial conversation?

A recommendation or a proposal for a focused next step. If the work needs more investigation, a discovery or prototype project can be scoped first. Any paid work is agreed before it begins.

What about ownership, hosting, and ongoing costs?

We set out source-code access, ownership, hosting accounts, data access, and recurring costs in the project scope. We also agree who will operate the software after handover.

What happens after delivery?

We agree on the handover, documentation, and any included support as part of the scope. Ongoing hosting, maintenance, and further development are discussed and priced as needed.
