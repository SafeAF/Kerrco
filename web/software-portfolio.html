IDWX / Weather & climate analysis

Weather analysis
suite.

Forecast comparisons, historical records,
and growing-season analytics.

Explore the forecast. Understand the local record.

Built around Twin Falls, IDWX combines current weather, forecast comparisons, and historical growing-season context. It makes temperature and precipitation patterns easier to explore, from the next two weeks to differences across years.

The challenge
Make different forecast sources and historical weather records useful together, while showing the coverage behind the data.
What I built
A weather analysis suite with forecast comparisons, historical freeze dates, growing-season context, and interactive graphs.
What you can do
Compare forecasts, explore differences across years, and inspect the values behind an analysis.

ActiveFundPerf / $CHPY ETF research

$CHPY ETF
research dashboard.

Holdings, trades, distributions,
and the evidence behind performance.

funds.kerrco.us

From public disclosures to useful research.

Researching $CHPY means connecting holdings files, trade records, and distribution history. ActiveFundPerf brings that evidence into a focused ETF research dashboard, with supporting sources and clear signals when data is incomplete.

The challenge
Connect holdings files, trade records, and distribution history into a research view with traceable supporting evidence.
What I built
A dashboard for $CHPY holdings, trade activity, distribution-adjusted returns, and supported performance analysis.
What you can do
Explore reported positions and activity, inspect the source records, and see when evidence is missing or incomplete.

order.kerrco.us

A workflow shaped around canal operations.

Water delivery involves more than choosing a date. This application brings delivery requests, order status, and administrator review into one place, with operational context from canal and headgate records.

The challenge
Represent requests by headgate, dates, and amounts alongside review and operational information.
What I built
Request forms, order-status views, administrator review screens, and canal flow-history charts and tables.
What the interface shows
Current and past requests, pending review, and recorded canal conditions in one application.
