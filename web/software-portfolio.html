Operations / Custom business software

Water ordering
& scheduling.

Software for the details
of irrigation-water delivery.

order.kerrco.us

A workflow shaped around canal operations.

Water delivery involves more than choosing a date. This application brings delivery requests, order status, and administrator review into one place, with operational context from canal and headgate records.

Requests & review
Headgate selection, requested dates and amounts, and a queue for reviewing pending requests.
Order visibility
Running, pending, rejected, and completed orders, with current and historical views.
Operational context
Canal capacity, operating status, recorded flows, and history charts and tables.

ActiveFundPerf.

and the evidence behind them.

funds.kerrco.us

From public disclosures to useful research.

Fund information is spread across holdings files, trade records, and distribution history. ActiveFundPerf brings that evidence into a focused dashboard, with supporting sources and clear signals when data is incomplete.

Holdings & exposures
Explore reported stock, option, and cash positions in one research workspace.
Trades & performance
Review CHPY trade activity, distribution-adjusted returns, and supported attribution analysis.
Traceable evidence
Inspect source records and disclosure dates, with missing or partial evidence kept visible.

Weather / Data exploration

IDWX.

Local weather,
with a longer view.

weather.kerrco.us

Explore the forecast. Understand the local record.

Built around Twin Falls, IDWX combines current weather, forecast comparisons, and historical growing-season context. It makes temperature and precipitation patterns easier to explore, from the next two weeks to differences across years.

Forecast comparison
Compare NWS, NOAA, and ECMWF guidance for temperature, rain, snow, and wind.
Growing-season context
Explore spring and fall freeze dates, frost-free intervals, and the historical range behind them.
Interactive exploration
Compare years, build weather graphs, and inspect the source values and coverage behind each view.

Historical context is labeled separately from current conditions and forecasts.
