# KERRCO Website Revamp Plan

Goal:
Revamp kerrco.us from a generic construction/framing site into a focused local portfolio site for:

1. Construction
2. Architectural woodwork
3. Technical systems / business software

Core positioning:
KERRCO builds physical and technical systems for businesses, builders, and property owners in the Magic Valley.

Avoid presenting the company as "we do everything." Present it as a rare combination of hands-on construction skill, fine woodworking/craftsmanship, and practical software/system-building ability.

Do NOT lead with "AI consulting." If AI is mentioned, frame it as practical business automation, reporting, dashboards, and internal tools.

Tone:
- Local
- Competent
- Direct
- Not corporate
- Not startup-bro
- Not SEO spam
- Trustworthy and field-tested

Visual style:
- Clean, dark/navy construction-industrial palette
- White/light content sections
- Strong project photography
- Gold/yellow accent can remain
- Avoid stock icons where possible
- Use real photos as proof
- Portfolio-first

Site structure:

#### AI and Operations Consulting

Add a subsection under Technical Systems for local businesses that need help applying AI or automation to everyday operations.

Do not headline this as "AI consulting" on its own. Position it as:
"AI-assisted operations and internal tools"

Purpose:
Help local businesses identify where AI, automation, dashboards, or custom software can remove repetitive work, improve visibility, and support better decisions.

Include examples:
- turning paper forms, spreadsheets, or text messages into organized workflows
- summarizing job notes, service calls, inspections, or customer requests
- building internal search over company documents, policies, manuals, or records
- automating recurring reports
- routing leads, work orders, or customer requests to the right person
- creating dashboards for owners and managers
- connecting disconnected tools where possible
- building lightweight assistants for office staff or field teams

Copy direction:
Keep this grounded. The audience is local contractors, service companies, agricultural operations, property managers, small manufacturers, and other owner-operated businesses. They may not care about AI as a technology category. They care about fewer dropped balls, less duplicate entry, faster answers, and clearer operations.

Suggested copy:
"For local businesses, AI is useful when it is tied to real work. KERRCO helps identify practical places where automation, reporting, or AI-assisted tools can reduce manual entry, organize information, and make day-to-day operations easier to manage."

Avoid:
- "transform your business with AI"
- "cutting-edge AI strategy"
- "AI-powered innovation"
- vague chatbot-first language
- promising full automation of complex judgment work

Prefer:
- "reduce manual entry"
- "make information easier to find"
- "turn messy workflows into usable systems"
- "help staff spend less time chasing details"
- "build tools around how the business actually works"

#### Bespoke Business Software

Add a clear subsection for custom software built around a specific local business workflow.

Positioning:
"Custom software for businesses that have outgrown spreadsheets, paper, or disconnected tools."

Include examples:
- order and scheduling systems
- customer/job tracking
- inventory or asset tracking
- field reporting
- estimating or quote workflows
- document generation
- owner dashboards
- inspection/checklist tools
- simple CRM-style systems for niche local operations

Suggested copy:
"Some businesses do not need a giant software platform. They need a focused tool that matches how their work actually moves. KERRCO can build bespoke internal software for scheduling, tracking, reporting, quoting, or other workflows where off-the-shelf tools do not fit."

Important distinction:
Bespoke software should be presented as part of Technical Systems, not as a separate fourth company identity. It should support the broader positioning that KERRCO builds practical physical and technical systems.
